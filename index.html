Carlton Kigonya, Tanaka Chirara, and Yunting Yin

Clean Cooking Research and Ubuntu Clean Cooking Alliance

Over four years, I studied deaths linked to indoor air pollution from household cooking and what makes smoke exposure hard to reduce.

Read more

That research led me to explore a low-cost stove and vent. With Dr. Stephen Hubbard’s guidance, I adapted the design for locally available materials. The installation work in Marondera, Zimbabwe, led me to propose the Ubuntu Clean Cooking Alliance, an open-source platform where communities could share and adapt illustrated stove-building guides for locally available materials.
